1. Executive summary
Rating: Not ready
The core storefront is in good shape: linting, type checking, all 896 tests, and a launch-configured production build passed. Checkout prices and stock are server-authoritative, webhook signatures are verified, order creation and stock reduction are concurrency-safe, private database tables are protected, and the live storefront behaved correctly across desktop and mobile checks.
Launch is blocked by two confirmed code issues:
The application does not enforce Stripe sandbox mode. A mistakenly configured live secret would enable real charges, and live-mode webhook events are accepted.
Every successful full refund automatically returns all items to sellable inventory, even when products are damaged, lost, opened, or otherwise unsuitable for resale.

2. Critical launch blockers
C1 — Stripe sandbox mode is not enforced
Severity: Critical
Evidence: [src/lib/stripe/server.ts (line 3)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/lib/stripe/server.ts:3), [src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts (line 825)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts:825)
What is wrong: The Stripe client accepts any non-empty secret. There is no sk_test_ check, and webhook processing does not reject event.livemode === true.
Why it matters: A Vercel configuration mistake could switch the store from sandbox to real charging, contrary to the explicit business requirement. A matching live webhook secret would also let live events create orders and change inventory.
Recommended fix: Fail startup/build when STRIPE_SECRET_KEY is not a test key, reject live-mode webhook events, and add automated tests for both guards. Confirm production and preview Vercel environments contain only sandbox credentials.
Current state: The local secret was confirmed to identify as test mode; external Vercel configuration could not be verified.
C2 — Full refunds automatically restock potentially unsellable goods
Severity: Critical
Evidence: [src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts (line 453)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts:453), [20260722020000_add_refund_stock_restoration.sql (line 20)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/supabase/migrations/20260722020000_add_refund_stock_restoration.sql:20)
What is wrong: Any succeeded refund whose individual amount equals the order total calls restore_order_stock_after_refund. The SQL adds every order-item quantity back to products.stock_quantity without checking return receipt, condition, hygiene eligibility, loss, damage, or individual lines.
Why it matters: Refunded perfume, skincare, or beauty goods may be opened, damaged, lost, or unsafe to resell. Treating them as available stock can cause overselling and incorrect fulfilment.
Recommended fix: Separate financial refund status from inventory disposition. Require an inspected, per-line restock decision, with an explicit reason and audit trail. Only automatically restock clearly safe cases such as a pre-dispatch cancellation.
3. High-priority issues
H1 — Failed transactional emails have no automatic or manual retry path
Evidence: [src/lib/email/order-emails.ts (line 193)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/lib/email/order-emails.ts:193), [src/app/admin/(dashboard)/operations/page.tsx (line 377)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/admin/(dashboard\)/operations/page.tsx:377)
Problem: Failed sends are recorded and swallowed so Stripe receives success, which is correct, but nothing schedules another attempt. The operations page is explicitly read-only.
Impact: Customers or the seller can permanently miss order/refund notifications after a temporary Resend outage.
Fix: Add a scheduled queue worker or authenticated retry action using the existing claim and idempotency mechanism. Alert when retries are exhausted.
H2 — The shipping policy promises tracking details, but no shipping email exists
Evidence: [shipping-policy/page.tsx (line 41)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/(storefront\)/shipping-policy/page.tsx:41), [src/app/admin/actions.ts (line 58)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/admin/actions.ts:58), [src/lib/email/order-emails.ts (line 255)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/lib/email/order-emails.ts:255)
Problem: Admin fulfilment updates store courier and tracking information, but do not trigger a customer email. Email kinds cover orders/refunds, not dispatch.
Impact: The store cannot reliably fulfil its customer-facing tracking promise.
Fix: Add an idempotent customer_shipped email triggered by the first dispatched state, or revise the policy and establish a documented manual process.
H3 — Inventory controls are display-only
Evidence: [src/lib/admin/inventory-data.ts (line 39)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/lib/admin/inventory-data.ts:39), [src/app/admin/(dashboard)/inventory/page.tsx](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/admin/(dashboard\)/inventory/page.tsx)
Problem: Admin inventory reads and filters stock, but there is no audited stock-adjustment action or RPC.
Impact: Stock receipts, breakage, supplier corrections, and safe partial-return adjustments require direct database access.
Fix: Add a concurrency-safe inventory-adjustment RPC and authenticated UI requiring quantity delta, reason, operator, and immutable history.
H4 — Partial refunds cannot be reconciled correctly
Evidence: [src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts (line 479)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts:479)
Problem: Each refund is compared individually to the order total. Multiple partial refunds that cumulatively reach the full amount never become a fully refunded order. Partial-refund events are recorded as permanent manual failures.
Impact: Refund status and stock can remain unresolved indefinitely, especially because inventory adjustments are absent.
Fix: Maintain a refund ledger and use Stripe’s aggregate refunded amount, while recording per-line inventory disposition separately.
H5 — Coupons can be reused without limit
Evidence: [20260724010000_add_discount_codes.sql (line 1)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/supabase/migrations/20260724010000_add_discount_codes.sql:1)
Problem: Coupons have no total redemption limit, per-customer limit, minimum spend, or redemption audit record.
Impact: A leaked active code can be reused indefinitely and materially reduce margin.
Fix: Add atomic redemption tracking and configurable usage limits, or explicitly approve unlimited reuse as a business decision.
H6 — Legal operator and courier details remain unresolved
Evidence: [src/lib/legal/business-details.ts (line 3)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/lib/legal/business-details.ts:3), [terms/page.tsx (line 14)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/(storefront\)/terms/page.tsx:14)
Problem: Legal name, registration number, address, and courier are placeholders. The Terms identify only “Sombre” as the operator.
Impact: Customers may not know the contracting business or return address, and the operational/legal sufficiency of the policies cannot be confirmed.
Fix: Obtain appropriate Hong Kong legal review, finalize the operating identity and return address, and update the policy pages before accepting orders.
H7 — www.sombrebeauty.com does not resolve
Evidence: Live DNS/browser check; apex sombrebeauty.com works, while www.sombrebeauty.com returned ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED.
Problem: No working www record or redirect was found.
Impact: Visitors using the common www hostname receive a hard failure.
Fix: Add the Cloudflare DNS record and permanent redirect to the canonical apex domain, then verify HTTPS certificate coverage.
H8 — Supplier-after-order fulfilment is not modelled
Evidence: [products/[slug\]/page.tsx (line 76)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/(storefront\)/products/[slug]/page.tsx:76), [shipping-policy/page.tsx (line 33)](/Users/harrysocool/Desktop/sombre/src/app/(storefront\)/shipping-policy/page.tsx:33)
Problem: Availability is based entirely on stored stock_quantity, and the storefront presents positive quantities as in stock. There is no supplier availability, purchase-order, preorder, backorder, or lead-time state.
Impact: If goods are purchased from a supplier only after checkout, displayed availability and the 3–7-day processing promise can become inaccurate.
Fix: Either expose only physically/firmly reserved stock or implement an explicit supplier/preorder workflow with availability checks and appropriate customer copy.
